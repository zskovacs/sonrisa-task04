# Product brief

## Product need

Users should be able to set up alerts and receive notifications when something important happens in the world. Breaking news, market movements, and natural disasters are examples of potential subjects, not a commitment to support all three in the first version.

Email and Slack delivery are required. The product should allow additional notification channels later. An admin view is also required.

## What is not specified

The brief is intentionally incomplete. There are no agreed wireframes, schemas, event sources, event-detection rules, or definition of “important.” User workflows, rule semantics, access expectations, admin capabilities, scale, and retention are not defined.

Do not infer requirements to fill these gaps. Track assumptions and open questions in [the separate register](02-assumptions-and-open-questions.md); distinguish proposed scope from agreed product needs.

## Delivery constraint

The work is time-boxed, with no agreed numeric duration recorded here. Prioritize useful behavior, simplicity, explicit trade-offs, maintainability, and a demonstrable end-to-end path over feature count.

The initial [scope proposal](03-scope.md) and [engineering plan](01-plan.md) describe how to narrow the work. They do not make unanswered product questions into requirements.
